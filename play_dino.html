Dino Run — single game

A 5×10 grid of cubes shifts one column toward the dino every 400ms; a fresh decision is asked every tick, not just when something is close. The dino sits in the leftmost column, 2nd row from the bottom, and moves up on jump / down on duck.

Rules: 🌵 cactus needs jump · 🐦 low bird needs duck · 🦅 high bird needs none (run). Whatever sits at distance 0 arrives this tick — the wrong action for it is a crash. Row height is illustrative (cactus low, low-bird mid, high-bird top); the pass/fail rule is always by obstacle type, not row overlap.
