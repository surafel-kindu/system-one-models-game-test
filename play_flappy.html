Flappy Bird — single game

A 10×12 grid shifts one column left every 400ms and a fresh flap-or-not decision is asked every tick. Unlike Dino Run's lookup rule, this is real physics: the bird has a row and a vertical speed, and pipes, the ceiling and the floor all end the run.

🐦 flap = rise 2 rows and reset the fall; do nothing = fall faster (up to 2 rows per tick). The bird must be inside the pipe's gap on the tick the pipe reaches its column.
